# GreenLeaf Product Warranty

GreenLeaf products include a limited 24-month warranty against manufacturing defects from the original purchase date.

Warranty claims require a receipt or other valid proof of purchase. Accidental damage, normal wear, liquid damage, and unauthorized modifications are excluded.

Customers should contact support with the order number, product name, purchase date, and a description of the issue. Approved replacements are shipped after the claim is reviewed.
