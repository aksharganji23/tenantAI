# SaaSsum AI Demo Questions

Use these questions after uploading the sample documents:

1. What are Acme Support's response targets?
2. What is the refund window in the Acme handbook?
3. How many vacation days does Northstar provide?
4. What is the GreenLeaf product warranty period?
5. What information is needed for a warranty claim?
6. Ask about a fact that is not in the uploaded document and confirm that the assistant declines to invent an answer.
