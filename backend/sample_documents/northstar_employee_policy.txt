# Northstar Employee Policy

Northstar employees receive 20 paid vacation days per calendar year. Vacation requests should be submitted at least five working days before the planned start date when practical.

The company laptop replacement cycle is three years. Lost or stolen equipment must be reported to IT and Security within four hours of discovery.

Confidential company information may be accessed only for authorized business purposes. Employees must use multi-factor authentication and must not upload confidential documents to personal services.
