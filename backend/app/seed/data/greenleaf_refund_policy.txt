# GreenLeaf Retail & Organic Market - Customer Refund & Return Policy

## Section 1: Standard Return Window & Requirements
GreenLeaf Retail is dedicated to providing sustainably sourced, premium organic products and wellness goods.
- Return Window: Customers may return non-perishable eligible merchandise within 30 days of purchase date with the original printed or digital sales receipt.
- Condition: Items must be unused, unwashed, in their original packaging, and with all protective manufacturer seals intact.
- Proof of Purchase: A verified GreenLeaf physical receipt, mobile app transaction barcode, or loyalty member phone number lookup is required for all returns.

## Section 2: Non-Refundable & Perishable Goods Exceptions
For strict customer hygiene, sanitary safety, and public health compliance reasons, the following categories are strictly non-refundable and cannot be returned under any circumstances:
- Perishable groceries including fresh organic produce, dairy, bakery items, refrigerated kombucha, and raw meats.
- Opened cosmetics, skincare balms, essential oil tinctures, and body care products where the tamper seal has been unsealed.
- Personalized gift baskets, customized floral bouquets, and special event catering orders.
- Digital and physical GreenLeaf gift cards.

## Section 3: Restocking Fees for Appliances and Electronics
Small kitchen appliances, slow cold-press juicers, countertop blenders, and ultrasonic aroma diffusers returned within the 30-day window:
- If unopened in factory packaging: 100% full refund with no fee.
- If opened, tested, or unsealed: Subject to a mandatory 15% restocking and sterilization fee deducted from the refunded amount, unless inspected and confirmed to have an out-of-box manufacturer defect.

## Section 4: Refund Methods and Processing Times
- Credit and Debit Cards: Refunds are reversed to the original payment card within 3 to 5 business days, subject to the card issuing bank's clearing cycle.
- Cash Purchases: Cash returns under $100 are refunded immediately at customer service. Returns exceeding $100 may be issued via bank transfer or GreenLeaf store credit.
- GreenLeaf Store Credit: Issued instantly at checkout, usable across all retail locations and online, and never expires.
- Promotional Bundles: If one component of a "Buy One, Get One" (BOGO) or bundle discount is returned, the remaining retained items revert to regular non-discounted pricing.
